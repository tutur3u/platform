# Cinematic Web Design

Use this direction for expressive marketing or editorial work. Fit the layout to
its content and audience; AIDA can help conversion pages but is not a dashboard template.
Choose typography and composition deliberately. Never fabricate script execution,
random choices, measurements, or visual verification.

Prefer a clear hero, a recognizable primary action, and readable typography at
phone and small-laptop sizes. Adjust wrapping to actual copy and localization.
Use balanced grids when content belongs in a grid; preserve DOM reading order.

GSAP ScrollTrigger can support pinned storytelling, restrained text reveals, or
card stacking when the brief benefits from them. Use the project's existing motion
stack where possible, clean up timelines and triggers, and provide a usable static
experience for reduced motion and without animation initialization. Keep interaction
responsive and avoid scroll hijacking.

Use real project assets or clearly identified placeholders. Verify images resolve
and match the content. Preserve the project's fonts, icons, and theme tokens unless
the redesign includes changing them. Evaluate the resulting page at relevant
viewports; iterate on visible problems, not an arbitrary number of animation effects.
