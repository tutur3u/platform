# Complete Artifacts

Use for explicitly requested full files, exhaustive lists, or unabridged deliverables.
Deliver the requested content without placeholder implementations or omitted sections.
Existing TODOs, language spread syntax, and illustrative ellipses are not defects by themselves.

For workspace tasks, write complete files and link to them; a concise final response
need not repeat their contents. Continue through the requested implementation and
verification. If a tool or output limit prevents completion, identify exactly what
remains and retain a resumable artifact rather than inventing a routine review pause.
