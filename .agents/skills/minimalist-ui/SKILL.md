# Warm Editorial Minimalism

Use a restrained visual language when it fits the requested brand: warm neutral
surfaces, clear typographic contrast, quiet dividers, and sparse semantic accents.
Preserve established fonts, icons, and tokens unless redesigning them is in scope.

Let content determine density and spacing. Open editorial sections, compact data
rows, or flat cards are options; do not force all content into a bento layout.
Use real copy and assets, identify mock data, and avoid decorative controls that
imply nonexistent functionality.

Keep body text readable, secondary text sufficiently contrasted, and controls
recognizable without color alone. Preserve focus indicators, keyboard operation,
and meaningful empty/error/loading states. Adapt the composition at narrow widths
and enlarged text sizes.

Motion is optional. Use brief state transitions where helpful and respect reduced
motion; do not hide content until an animation succeeds. Verify the implemented
surface against the intended hierarchy and the actual user task.
