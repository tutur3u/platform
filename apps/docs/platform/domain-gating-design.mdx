# Workspace domains: access and abuse controls

## Status

The product decisions below are accepted. Slices 1 to 4 are implemented in code;
billing checkout stays behind a server flag and rollout (slice 5) has not started.
Operator setup lives in the [workspace domains runbook](/platform/workspace-domains-runbook).

## Decisions

**Scope:** a workspace binds hostnames to its public web presence: apex custom
domains (`example.com`), custom subdomains (`blog.example.com`) and platform
subdomains (`<label>.<apex>`). The platform apex and the custom-domain fallback
origin are configuration (`WORKSPACE_DOMAINS_PLATFORM_APEX`,
`WORKSPACE_DOMAINS_FALLBACK_ORIGIN`; production `ttr.gg` and `saas.ttr.gg`).
Either unset disables that kind of domain. A domain serves the workspace's published CMS site
(External Projects delivery rendered with `@tuturuuu/cms-blocks`). Without an
enabled canonical project it serves a public landing page with the workspace name,
avatar and a "Powered by Tuturuuu" link, and nothing private.

**Default:** deny. A workspace can add a domain only when one of these holds:

1. **Paid add-on.** The workspace is on a paid plan (tier is not `FREE`) and holds
   an active custom-domain add-on whose quantity covers its domains. The add-on
   costs **$10 per domain per month**. One add-on unit is one domain.
2. **Staff bypass.** The acting user is verified Tuturuuu staff and holds
   `manage_workspace_domains` in that workspace. Verified staff means a current
   Auth user with a confirmed email in exactly `@tuturuuu.com`
   (`isVerifiedTuturuuuStaffSession` with `allowXwf: false`, and the SQL twin
   `public.is_verified_tuturuuu_staff(user, false)`). Subdomain mailboxes such as
   `xwf.tuturuuu.com` and lookalike domains never qualify. No other bypass exists.

The bypass never skips hostname validation, ownership proof, caps, the kill switch
or audit. A staff-bypass domain keeps serving only while its creator is still
verified staff.

**Billing launch gate:** paid add-ons ship with no new charge. Checkout appears only
when `WORKSPACE_DOMAINS_BILLING_ENABLED=true` in apps/web, and add-on entitlement
counts only when `private.workspace_domain_settings.paid_addon_enabled` is true.
Until both are on, paid workspaces see the price with a "coming soon" note and only
the staff bypass can activate domains. Billing goes through the existing Polar
subscription path; the add-on is a Polar product with metadata
`product_kind: "custom_domain_addon"` reconciled into
`private.workspace_domain_addons`, never into plan tables.

**Permissions:** `view_workspace_domains` reads domains and entitlement;
`manage_workspace_domains` adds, verifies and removes them. Workspace creators and
admins hold both through `has_workspace_permission`.

**Limits:** 10 domains per workspace by default (`default_domain_cap`), with a root
override in `private.workspace_domain_caps`. A lapsed add-on keeps serving for 7 days
after its period end (`lapse_grace_days`), oldest domains first, then stops; new
domains are blocked immediately. Removed platform subdomains are quarantined from
other workspaces for 30 days (`quarantine_days`); a removed custom hostname can be
claimed by another workspace only by passing a fresh ownership TXT check.

**Reserved names:** the managed-project rule (`tuturuuu.com`, `tutur3u.com` and
their subdomains, released domains) plus `tuturuu.com`, the `ttr.gg` apex, nested
`ttr.gg` names and reserved labels such as `www`, `saas`, `fysh`, `api` and `admin`
(`packages/utils/src/workspace-domain-hostnames.ts`). The bootstrap hosts
`vhp.ttr.gg` (also a reserved label) and `vohoangphuc.com` are bound to their
workspace by the seed migration `20261111120200`, so no other workspace can
claim them.

### Lifecycle

1. **Add** (`POST /api/v1/workspaces/:wsId/domains`): normalize and classify the
   hostname, check entitlement (fails closed), insert a `pending_verification` row
   with a random per-domain TXT token, audit. The insert trigger enforces the cap,
   kill switch and quarantine under a per-workspace lock; a partial unique index
   keeps one live row per hostname across all workspaces.
2. **Verify** (`POST .../domains/:id/verify`, idempotent): re-check entitlement,
   then for custom hostnames look up `_tuturuuu-verify.<host>` over DNS-over-HTTPS.
   With proof, create or refresh the Cloudflare custom hostname and show its DCV
   records. When the certificate is active, attach the `<host>/*` Worker route and
   mark the domain `active`.

   Platform subdomains have no ownership proof, so the zone itself is the proof
   of absence: before the row is created and again before provisioning, any DNS
   record at the name (all types), a covering wildcard record (`*.<parent>`) or a
   Workers route whose pattern matches the host (exact, `*.ttr.gg/*`, `*ttr.gg/*`,
   `*/*`) refuses the name with `hostname_in_use`. The proxied record and the
   route are created together; a failure or create conflict (DNS 81053/81057,
   route 10020, any "already exists") deletes what that attempt created and
   leaves no provider IDs on the row. Custom hostnames refuse an existing
   matching route the same way.
3. **Serve:** the edge Worker resolves the host through
   `GET /api/v1/workspace-domains/resolve` and reads published content from
   `GET /api/v1/workspace-domains/site`. Both require the edge secret and answer
   only for `active` domains that `private.workspace_domain_serving_allowed`
   still entitles. A resolver 404 is final at the edge.
4. **Remove** (`DELETE .../domains/:id`): detach the Worker route, DNS record and
   custom hostname (best effort; failures are recorded), then mark `removed`.

Audit rows in `private.workspace_domain_audit` record actor, workspace, hostname,
action, entitlement source and outcome, never DNS tokens or provider credentials.

  behavior in Polar, and tax treatment.
- Rate limits for verification beyond the shared session rate limit, and provider
  budget alerts for custom hostname issuance.
- `ttr project new` integration and email sender-domain verification reuse.
