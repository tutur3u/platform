## Usage

Language helps you view everything on [Tuturuuu](https://tuturuuu.com) in your preferred language.

### Supported Languages

Currently, we provide first-class support for the following languages:

- **English**
- **Vietnamese**

### How to Change Language

[Tuturuuu](https://tuturuuu.com) detects your browser's language automatically. To choose one yourself, open the account menu from your avatar and pick **Language**: **English**, **Tiếng Việt** or **System**. Signed-out visitors use the language toggle in the public navigation bar.

- Picking a language stores it in the `NEXT_LOCALE` cookie. At an app's own host the cookie is shared across Tuturuuu subdomains, so every app follows it.
- **System** removes that choice. The app then follows your browser's languages (`Accept-Language`), falling back to English.
- The menu marks the stored choice, or **System** when there is none, whatever locale the current URL shows.
- After a change the page loads again as a full document under the chosen locale's prefix (`/vi/...`, `/en/...`); for **System**, under your browser's language. A locale prefix outranks the cookie, so this is the one load every app's proxy renders in the chosen language. Apps that serve a locale unprefixed redirect there. A client refresh cannot do this on the native Start runtime, whose loaders skip the proxy's locale negotiation.

Exceptions:

- Web and Workspace public pages are English at unprefixed addresses and Vietnamese under `/vi`, whatever the cookie says, so search engines see one fixed locale per address.
- An app mounted under `tuturuuu.com/<app>` stores its choice only under its own path. **System** there clears that choice and then follows the choice made on tuturuuu.com, when one exists, rather than the browser.

Scope: Web, Workspace, Nova and every satellite app's account menu (`LanguageDropdownItem` and `SystemLanguageDropdownItem` in `@tuturuuu/ui`) and the public `LanguageToggle`. Colab keeps its own locale store with the same three choices. Coverage: `locale-preference.test.ts`, `language-dropdown-item.test.tsx` and `language-toggle.test.tsx` under `packages/ui/src/components/ui/custom/`.

### Contribute

If you want to help us translate [Tuturuuu](https://tuturuuu.com) into your language, feel free to open a pull request on our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/tutur3u/platform).

Each supported locale lives in a single message bundle under `apps/web/messages/`:

- `apps/web/messages/en.json` for English
- `apps/web/messages/vi.json` for Vietnamese

When you add or change a key, update it in **both** `en.json` and `vi.json` so the bundles stay in sync, then run `bun i18n:sort` to keep the keys ordered consistently before committing. `bun i18n:sort` expands short inline arrays onto several lines; run `bun oxc:write` on the bundles you changed afterwards so the diff holds only your keys.
