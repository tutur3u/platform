## Usage

Themes are a way to customize the look and feel of your experience on [Tuturuuu](https://tuturuuu.com). You can choose from a variety of themes to suit your preferences.

### Modes

Modes are how the entire website looks. You can choose between **light mode**, **dark mode**, and **system mode** (which follows your system settings).

The shared web light theme uses `--muted-foreground: hsl(0 0% 40%)` for supporting text. Against white (`#FFFFFF`), this provides approximately 5.74:1 contrast, increasing the WCAG AA margin for normal text from approximately 4.74:1 at 45.1% lightness. This default applies to web surfaces using the shared token; custom palettes and the independent mobile theme retain their own colors. The dark theme remains `hsl(0 0% 63.9%)`.

### Basic theme switcher

When you are not logged in, a basic theme switcher will be available which will allow you to switch between standard **dark mode** and **light mode** by clicking on the theme switcher in the top right corner of our website.

### Advanced theme switcher

You can switch between a variety of themes by tapping on your profile picture in the top right corner of our website and selecting the **Theme** option.

<Tip>This switcher is will available when you are logged in.</Tip>

#### Variants

When using **Advanced theme switcher**, you can also choose a variant of the selected mode.

<Note>For example, you can choose dark mode with a blue accent.</Note>

Available variants:

- **Standard** (Default)
- **Pink**
- **Purple**
- **Yellow**
- **Orange**
- **Green**
- **Blue**

## Experimental design (Sorbet)

Sorbet is the next Tuturuuu design: a warm white ground, white surfaces, an ink
primary colour, pastel fills with matching inks, pill-shaped controls, 18px cards,
soft shadows and the Nunito typeface. You can try it before it becomes the default.

- **Where:** Settings → Appearance → **Experimental design**, in every web app that
  ships the shared settings dialog (Tuturuuu web and workspace, and the satellite
  apps such as Calendar, Tasks, Finance and Drive).
- **Default:** off. The switch is saved to your account (user config
  `EXPERIMENTAL_SORBET_UI`), so turning it on in one app turns it on in every app
  you use with that account. Other apps pick up the change when you return to
  them; other tabs of the same app follow at once.
- **Light and dark:** Sorbet has both, and still follows your mode choice above.
  Every text colour keeps at least 4.5:1 contrast in both, including white
  text on solid status fills in dark mode. While Sorbet is on, the Light, Dark
  and System tiles under Appearance preview your palette's light and dark sides
  (`settings/theme-mode-preview.test.tsx`).
- **Palettes:** while Sorbet is on, **Palette** under the switch offers Sorbet
  (the default), Matcha, Blueberry, Mango, Lavender, Lagoon, Rosé, Graphite,
  Midnight and High contrast. Each has a light and a dark side. The choice is
  saved to your account (user config `SORBET_THEME`) and follows you across apps
  like the switch. High contrast keeps every text colour at 7:1 or more, with
  solid borders and a wider focus ring.
- **Corners:** **Corners** under the palette offers Soft (the default), Sharp
  and Round. Soft keeps 12px rows, 18px cards and pill controls; Sharp uses
  6px rows, 10px cards and 8px controls; Round uses 16px rows, 24px cards and
  pill controls. Sheets, panels and menus follow the same choice. It is saved
  in the same account theme as the palette. `sorbet-tokens.test.ts` checks
  each option against the contract's `radius.corners` scale.
- **Signed out:** public pages keep the last choices made on that device and
  never read the account.
- **Exception, the public roadmap:** `tuturuuu.com/roadmap` uses Sorbet as its
  public design, so a visitor who has never set the switch sees Sorbet. A device
  whose switch is off gets the classic design, applied before the first paint
  (the roadmap's boot script), and a palette chosen on the device still applies.
  The roadmap's own colours read Sorbet's role names (`--primary`, `--card`,
  `--ring` and the status inks), never a palette's source colours.
- **Mobile app:** the same switch appears under Settings → Appearance →
  **Experimental design**, with Palette and Corners while it is on. It reads and
  writes the same two account settings, so turning Sorbet on in the app turns it
  on in the web apps and back again. See [Mobile app](#mobile-app).
- **Not covered:** Colab, and web apps without a theme provider
  (Hive, Tulletin, the link shortener and external embeds) keep their current
  design.

The switch only changes appearance. Pages, permissions and data stay the same.
How it works for engineers: `ExperimentalUiSync` (in `@tuturuuu/ui`) sets
`data-ui="sorbet"` on `<html>`, and `packages/ui/src/sorbet.css` remaps the
shared semantic tokens under that attribute. A palette other than Sorbet adds
`data-sorbet-preset`; `packages/ui/src/sorbet-presets.css` defines eleven source
colours per palette and mode and maps them onto the same tokens, so the default
palette renders exactly as `sorbet.css` alone. The stored theme is parsed
against an allowlist (`sorbet-theme.ts`), and any other value falls back to the
default. The sync reads both account values through the shared `user-config`
queries, so it mounts inside each app's `QueryClientProvider` and does not
retry a failed read. Where the provider knows nobody is signed in it passes
`account={false}`: the device values still apply, no query client is needed and
no account value is requested. So that a page opens in Sorbet rather than
switching after hydration, `SorbetPrePaintScript` (in `@tuturuuu/ui`) is mounted
beside each app's theme provider, outside any Suspense boundary. It is a static
inline script that reads only this device's stored values, sets only
allowlisted attributes on `<html>`, and takes the response's CSP nonce where an
app requires one; `sorbet-pre-paint.test.tsx` checks that it applies exactly
what the runtime applies for valid, invalid and oversized stored values.
The native Start apps (Calendar, Tasks, Mail, Meet and Apps) mount it with
their request nonce; Colab renders only on the client and does not apply
Sorbet. A policy without a per-request nonce allows the script by
`SORBET_PRE_PAINT_SCRIPT_HASH`, never `'unsafe-inline'`; a test recomputes that
hash, so any edit to the script fails until the hash and its policies change. Regression coverage lives in
`sorbet-ui.test.ts`, `sorbet-theme.test.ts`, `experimental-ui-sync.test.tsx` and
`settings/experimental-ui-setting.test.tsx` under
`packages/ui/src/components/ui/custom/`, and `packages/ui/src/sorbet-presets.test.ts`
checks every palette's contrast in both modes.

`packages/ui/src/sorbet-tokens.json` is the portable Sorbet token contract: the
colour roles and hue fills, inks and calm fills for both modes, type, spacing,
radius, shadows, control sizes, motion, elevation, mobile metrics and the
setting keys with their allowlists. Web reads it through `sorbet.css`; the
mobile app and the design system artifact map the same names. Changes to it are
additive. It also records the shared decisions both platforms follow:
calendar events use the calm fill of their hue while tags and chips use the
vibrant fill; the selected navigation item is `accent` with `foreground` ink;
skeletons use `surface-sunken`; the focus ring is 2px with a 2px offset (3px in
High Contrast); each Corners option (soft, sharp, round) has its own radius
scale for rows, cards, panels, controls, sheets and popovers; toasts are the
topmost layer (Sonner's own z-index), above dialogs, app overlays and the corner
badges, while the dock stays at 40 and overlays at 50; tablets use a
24px gutter with the dock capped at 480px; and every palette's eleven source
colours. `packages/ui/src/sorbet-tokens.test.ts` pins `sorbet.css` to the
contract and the palettes to `sorbet-presets.css`, keeps the setting allowlists
and popover corners equal to the runtime, and checks text and focus contrast.
The `classic` table gives the same colour roles and radius scale for the
default theme, read from `globals.css`, so the mobile app and the design canvas
draw both themes from one set of names. Classic `destructive` is a fill only, so
the table adds `destructive-ink` (`--red`) for destructive text; Sorbet's
`destructive` already reads as text. `sorbet-classic-tokens.test.ts` keeps the
table equal to `globals.css` and the dialog scrim and checks text contrast.

Toasts come from the shared Toaster (`@tuturuuu/ui/sonner`), so every app
gets the same skin from `floating-surface.css`. With Sorbet on, a toast floats
like a card (receding border, soft shadow, the popover corner), its type icon
sits in a pastel chip of its own ink, and its buttons are pill controls that
follow Corners. Bulk operations show `ToastProgress` (`@tuturuuu/ui/toast-progress`):
the label, x/N and an ink progress bar, replaced in the same toast by the
result and its Undo action. `floating-surface.test.tsx` keeps these rules on
tokens only.

`sorbet-surfaces.css` styles the shared shells (the settings dialog's
navigation, sections, rows and tabs) and `sorbet-components.css` the shared
primitives: accordion, breadcrumb, pagination, toggles and toggle groups,
progress, slider, skeleton, table, avatar, scroll area, navigation menu, alert
and the date picker. Selected pages and toggles take the `accent` fill; the
date picker rings today in ink; every one of these controls shows the solid
focus ring. Both files read only the shared tokens, so each palette and both
modes follow, and each has a test that keeps its rules under `data-ui`.

On phones (below the `md` breakpoint) with Sorbet on, the web dashboard shows
the mobile shell's floating dock (`MobileDock` in `@tuturuuu/ui`): Home, Tasks,
Calendar, Mira and Apps, matching the native app's dock. Destinations are
icon-only 52px slots named for assistive technology (`aria-label`, English
and Vietnamese), the current one in the `accent` fill. The 64px pill rests 8px
inside a home-indicator safe area, never closer than the 12px dock inset, with
its shadow falling below it. Mira opens `tuturuuu.com/personal` (the personal
workspace home) from every app; the retired `[wsId]/mira` pet module redirects
there. WebKit can resume a page (after an app switch, in Safari and
in in-app browsers such as Instagram's) with fixed content ending above the
visible bottom; `dock-viewport-anchor.ts` compares a hidden fixed probe with
`visualViewport` and moves the dock, its fade and bottom toasts back onto the
visible edge.
Below a destination's own page a detached circular Back button with its own
surface and shadow appears on the dock's leading side, on the pill's baseline;
a long press returns to that destination's page. Its lane is reserved on every
destination, so the pill keeps its place and size whether Back shows or not.
Apps opens the apps launcher. The top bar keeps the page title and actions and
never shows Back. Scrolling content reserves the dock's clearance, and a short
fade behind the dock keeps it legible. Every satellite app shows the same dock
(`SatelliteMobileDock`, mounted once in the shared satellite providers):
Tasks and Calendar mark their own slot, every other app keeps Apps active and
shows Back below its root, and links to other apps resolve their address for
the current environment. With Sorbet off, the classic mobile navigation is
unchanged. `mobile-dock.test.tsx` covers the active slot, icon-only
names, the detached Back lane, long press and the fixed pill; `dock-viewport-anchor.test.ts`
covers the resume correction; `dashboard-mobile-dock-location.test.ts` and
`satellite-mobile-dock-location.test.ts` cover which destination and Back
state each path gets.

On touch screens (`pointer: coarse`) Sorbet fields are at least 44px tall, and
buttons, tabs and switches get an invisible 44px hit area without changing their
drawn size. Bottom sheets and drawers round their top edge and pad for the home
indicator. Sheets and dialogs share one scrim. With Reduce Motion on, Sorbet's
motion durations drop to zero.

## Mobile app

In the mobile app, open **Settings → Appearance** to pick a mode (System, Light
or Dark), a theme (Classic, Aurora, Sunset, Ocean, Forest, Ember, Blossom or Mono),
an accent colour and a background style (Solid, Gradient or Blobs). For gradient
and blob backgrounds, you can tune intensity, blob softness and card opacity, and
optionally animate blobs. Animation stays off when your device's Reduce Motion
setting is on. Changes apply across the app immediately. **Reset to default**
restores Classic with the system mode.

**Experimental design** at the top of Appearance turns on Sorbet for your
account (off by default). It is the same switch as on the web
(`EXPERIMENTAL_SORBET_UI`), and its **Palette** and **Corners** choices are the
web's `SORBET_THEME` values, so both follow you across the app and the web apps.
An accent and density set on the web apply in the app too (Spacious renders as
Comfortable); the Nunito font choice renders in Noto Sans, which the app bundles. While Sorbet is on,
the classic theme, accent, background, density and corner controls are hidden
because Sorbet sets them; mode, interface style, haptics and Reduce Motion still
apply. The app remembers the last values per account on the device, so Sorbet
shows on the next launch before the account answers, and a change made offline
uploads when the app is back online without overwriting the other setting. The
app's Sorbet colours mirror `packages/ui/src/sorbet-tokens.json` and
`sorbet-presets.css`; `apps/mobile/test/core/theme/sorbet/sorbet_palette_parity_test.dart`
fails on any drift, and `sorbet_contrast_test.dart` checks every palette's text
contrast in both modes. See
[Mobile settings shell](/platform/features/mobile-settings-shell#settings-overview-and-appearance)
for defaults and behaviour.
