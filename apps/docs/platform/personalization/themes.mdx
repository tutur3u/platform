## Usage

Themes are a way to customize the look and feel of your experience on [Tuturuuu](https://tuturuuu.com). You can choose from a variety of themes to suit your preferences.

### Modes

Modes are how the entire website looks. You can choose between **light mode**, **dark mode**, and **system mode** (which follows your system settings).

The shared web light theme uses `--muted-foreground: hsl(0 0% 40%)` for supporting text. Against white (`#FFFFFF`), this provides approximately 5.74:1 contrast, increasing the WCAG AA margin for normal text from approximately 4.74:1 at 45.1% lightness. This default applies to web surfaces using the shared token; custom palettes and the independent mobile theme retain their own colors. The dark theme remains `hsl(0 0% 63.9%)`.

### Basic theme switcher

When you are not logged in, a basic theme switcher will be available which will allow you to switch between standard **dark mode** and **light mode** by clicking on the theme switcher in the top right corner of our website.

### Advanced theme switcher

You can switch between a variety of themes by tapping on your profile picture in the top right corner of our website and selecting the **Theme** option.

<Tip>This switcher is will available when you are logged in.</Tip>

#### Variants

When using **Advanced theme switcher**, you can also choose a variant of the selected mode.

<Note>For example, you can choose dark mode with a blue accent.</Note>

Available variants:

- **Standard** (Default)
- **Pink**
- **Purple**
- **Yellow**
- **Orange**
- **Green**
- **Blue**

## Experimental design (Sorbet)

Sorbet is the next Tuturuuu design: a warm white ground, white surfaces, an ink
primary colour, pastel fills with matching inks, pill-shaped controls, 18px cards,
soft shadows and the Nunito typeface. You can try it before it becomes the default.

- **Where:** Settings → Appearance → **Experimental design**, in every web app that
  ships the shared settings dialog (Tuturuuu web and workspace, and the satellite
  apps such as Calendar, Tasks, Finance and Drive).
- **Default:** off. The switch is saved to your account (user config
  `EXPERIMENTAL_SORBET_UI`), so turning it on in one app turns it on in every app
  you use with that account. Other apps pick up the change when you return to
  them; other tabs of the same app follow at once.
- **Light and dark:** Sorbet has both, and still follows your mode choice above.
  Every text colour keeps at least 4.5:1 contrast in both.
- **Signed out:** public pages keep the last choice made on that device.
- **Not covered:** the mobile app, Colab, and web apps without a theme provider
  (Hive, Tulletin, the link shortener and external embeds) keep their current
  design.

The switch only changes appearance. Pages, permissions and data stay the same.
How it works for engineers: `ExperimentalUiSync` (in `@tuturuuu/ui`) sets
`data-ui="sorbet"` on `<html>`, and `packages/ui/src/sorbet.css` remaps the
shared semantic tokens under that attribute. Regression coverage lives in
`sorbet-ui.test.ts`, `experimental-ui-sync.test.tsx` and
`settings/experimental-ui-setting.test.tsx` under
`packages/ui/src/components/ui/custom/`.

## Mobile app

In the mobile app, open **Settings → Appearance** to pick a mode (System, Light
or Dark), a theme (Classic, Aurora, Sunset, Ocean, Forest, Ember, Blossom or Mono),
an accent colour and a background style (Solid, Gradient or Blobs). For gradient
and blob backgrounds, you can tune intensity, blob softness and card opacity, and
optionally animate blobs. Animation stays off when your device's Reduce Motion
setting is on. Changes apply across the app immediately. **Reset to default**
restores Classic with the system mode. See
[Mobile settings shell](/platform/features/mobile-settings-shell#settings-overview-and-appearance)
for defaults and behaviour.
