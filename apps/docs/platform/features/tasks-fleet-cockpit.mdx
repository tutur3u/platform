## What it is for

The Fleet view answers one question first: what is going on, and what needs me?
It is a read-only board view over a board's existing tasks. Slice 1 adds no
database migration. Cards are fed by the fleet:v1 card schema that the board
sync writes into each task.

## Scope and default

- **Where:** a board offers the Fleet view when any of its tasks carries a
  fleet:v1 block or a fleet label (`needs-user`, `state:`, `owner:` or `product:`).
  Such boards list Fleet first in the View menu, and it becomes the default view
  until the viewer picks another. The last choice is remembered per viewer, as for
  the other views. Boards without fleet tasks are unchanged.
- **Default triage:** the view opens on three buckets, in this order: **Needs me**,
  **Live today** and **Blocked**. A card that needs you is listed under Needs me
  even if it is also blocked. Queued, done and archived (closed) cards stay out of
  the view and are counted in the toolbar.
- **Grouping:** Triage (default), Product, Priority or Owner. Groups with a
  needs-you card come first, then groups with a blocked card, then the rest by
  size. Priority groups run P0 to P3. Empty groups are hidden.
- **Density:** comfortable by default; compact keeps one title line and one row
  of fields, for dense boards only.

## The card face

Every fleet card shows the same parts in the same places:

1. A scan row: product, priority as P0 to P3, and the task number.
2. A bounded title: a subject of at most 72 characters, clamped to two lines
   (one in compact). Longer titles are announced to screen readers as too long,
   not cut silently. Status prose, commit hashes and notes belong in fields or
   the description.
3. A **Needs you** callout with the one-line ask when `needs_user` is true. It is
   the only ink-filled element on a card. Otherwise a **Blocked by** line shows
   `blocked_by` when set.
4. Four fixed fields: status, owner, ETA and last update. A missing value is a
   word (**Unowned**, **No ETA**, **No update**), so data gaps stay visible. Last
   update never falls back to the created time.
5. Up to two links (PR, commit, thread, run, doc), then +N.

Status words always travel with an icon and a colour: needs you, running, in
review, CI running, blocked, done. Queued and unknown stay quiet. Inside the
fleet, blocked uses the red tone rather than the usual gray, because it is a stop
someone must clear.

Freshness comes from `last_update`: fresh under 2 hours, aging 2 to 24 hours,
stale at 24 hours or more. An ETA is due soon within 2 hours and overdue once
past, unless the card is done. Stale and overdue add their word, so colour is
never the only signal.

## The fleet:v1 contract

The board sync owns the data contract. The view reads a block at the top of the
task description, in markdown or a rich-text document:

```text
<!-- fleet:v1 -->
status: running | needs_user | waiting_review | waiting_ci | blocked | queued | done
owner: <slug>
product: <slug>
priority: critical | high | normal | low
eta: <ISO-8601>
last_update: <ISO-8601>
needs_user: true | false
needs_user_reason: <one line>
blocked_by: <one line>
links:
- pr: <url>
- commit: <sha>
<!-- /fleet:v1 -->
```

When a key is missing, the view falls back to:

- the labels `state:`, `owner:`, `product:` and `needs-user`;
- the task's priority;
- the task's end date for the ETA.

An explicit `needs_user: false` wins over the label.

## Surfaces

- **Web (Tasks app and every app that embeds the Tasks board):** the Fleet board
  view, with the summary strip (Needs me, Live today, Blocked, Stale 24h+). Clicking
  a card title opens the existing task side panel.
- **Mobile:** board tiles and list cards for fleet tasks use the same bounded
  title and structured fields. The task sheet shows the field grid, the needs-you
  callout and every link.
- **Design source:** the Tuturuuu design system's Fleet cockpit section and its
  FleetCard, FleetStatusChip, NeedsUserBadge, EtaIndicator, StalenessIndicator,
  DecisionCard, Timeline and CapacityPanel components. The web components live in
  `@tuturuuu/ui/custom/fleet`.

## Intentional exceptions and later slices

- Approve and steer decision cards, the activity timeline merged with fleet
  events, and the machines and capacity panel are designed but not in Slice 1.
  They arrive with the fleet event ingestion slice. Only `manage_mastermind`
  holders will be able to approve or steer.
- Done-card archiving after 24 hours is done by the board sync, not the view.
- Fleet is a board view (`?view=fleet`), not a new route, so the navigation menu
  is unchanged.
