title: "Task board sprints"
description: "Per-board sprints: turning them on, planning, starting, ending and the derived sprint due date"
---

## Scope and default

Sprints belong to one task board. A board has sprints off by default and keeps
today's views. A workspace member who can edit the board turns sprints on from
Board settings by picking a length of one or two weeks; Off turns them off again
and keeps every past sprint and report.

A sprint is a task cycle with a `board_id`. A board has at most one running
sprint and one sprint being planned. Sprints run Monday to Friday of their last
week, so the next sprint starts the Monday after the current one ends. Changing
the length applies to the sprint being planned and every later one.

## Membership

Turning sprints on puts every open task on the board into Sprint 1, the sprint
being planned. After that, a new task, or a task moved onto the board, joins the
running sprint (marked as added mid-sprint) or, when none is running, the sprint
being planned. A task sits in one open sprint per board; moving a task to
another board drops its open sprint. Done tasks stay in the sprint they
finished in. A task that becomes open again after its sprint ended (moved back
out of Done, un-closed, or restored from the recycle bin) rejoins the board's
current sprint the same way, so no open task drops out of the Board view or
Plan.

Regression coverage: `apps/database/supabase/tests/task-board-sprint-transitions.sql`
walks every way a task enters, leaves or returns to a board's sprints.

The backlog is not a separate list. It is the unassigned tasks of the sprint
being planned.

## Due dates

A task with no due date of its own is due when its sprint ends. That date is
derived and never written into `tasks.end_date`, so moving a sprint or carrying
a task rewrites nothing. Only an own due date can make a task overdue.

## Starting and ending

Starting and ending a sprint, and changing its length, are limited to workspace
members who can edit the board; board guests can plan (goals and membership)
but cannot start or end. Starting the planned sprint opens the next one for
planning. A sprint started before its planned Monday keeps its length: it
starts that day and ends on the Friday of its last week (a weekend start counts
from the next Monday), and the next sprint is planned after it.

Ending is atomic and idempotent. Every open task gets a decision: carry it into
the next sprint with its owner, or send it to the backlog, which removes its
assignees. A missing decision carries. The end stores a report: tasks in the
sprint, done, added mid-sprint, carried in from earlier sprints, carried on,
sent to the backlog, the same totals in estimation points, and per-person
totals. Ending twice returns the stored report.

Board sprints change only through the service-role RPCs
`set_board_sprint_length`, `start_board_sprint`, `end_board_sprint` and
`move_tasks_to_board_sprint`, called by the tasks API after its board-access
checks. Anonymous and signed-in sessions cannot execute them; the catalog check
in `apps/database/supabase/tests/20261027151200_anon-definer-execute.sql` lists
them as service-only. Direct row writes stay available for workspace-wide cycles
only, and the older task-cycle edit and delete routes refuse board sprints (404).
Every sprint writer locks the board row before its sprints, so moves, starts,
ends and task membership changes on one board run one at a time.

## Board header and Board view

With sprints on, the board header shows the current sprint after the board name:
a progress ring, the sprint dates and its done count. The current sprint is the
running one, or the sprint being planned when none runs. Its menu lists every
sprint in order; members who can edit the board also get Start for the planned
sprint (only while none runs) and a shortcut to the sprint length in Board
settings. The Board view (Kanban, List, Timeline) shows only the current
sprint's tasks. An open task no open sprint holds yet (just created, moved onto
the board, restored or reopened) shows at once, since the database has put it
in the current sprint; tasks waiting in the planned sprint and finished work
from past sprints stay hidden. My Tasks, Drafts and the Recycle Bin are unchanged and show no
sprint. With sprints off, or before the migration reaches a database, the header
and views look exactly as before.

## Exceptions

Workspace-wide cycles without a board keep their existing behavior. The
personal planner still writes `tasks.end_date` for the day it plans, so planner
tasks count as having their own due date.
