The Flutter app decides how a plan, credit or subscription purchase may start
through one seam in `apps/mobile/lib/core/platform/`:

- `purchase_channel.dart`: `PurchaseChannel`, `resolvePurchaseChannel`,
  `NativePurchaseAvailability`, `NativePurchaseLauncher` and `PurchaseScope`.
- `purchase_entry_point.dart`: `PurchaseEntryPoint`, the widget every upgrade,
  plan or credits call to action renders through.
- `purchase_links.dart`: the external purchase URL policy.

| Platform | Store available | Channel | Behavior |
|---|---|---|---|
| Android, Windows, Linux, web | n/a | `external` | Purchase links open the Tuturuuu web billing pages. |
| iOS, macOS | no (default) | `unavailable` | Calls to action are hidden; no external purchase link opens. |
| iOS, macOS | yes | `native` | Calls to action start `NativePurchaseLauncher.launch`; external purchase links still never open. |

App Store Guidelines 3.1.1 and 3.1.3 forbid the Apple builds from linking to a
purchase made outside StoreKit. The platform alone never selects `native`: a
`NativePurchaseAvailability` must report true, meaning the server verified store
purchases for the build and the store returned the products. Without a
`PurchaseScope`, availability is always false and the launcher is a no-op.

## Rules

- Paid entitlements a user already holds stay usable on every channel. Tier
  badges, credit balances and the current plan name are displays, not offers.
- Render purchase calls to action through `PurchaseEntryPoint`. Its first frame
  uses the synchronous `isAvailable` answer, then follows the `changes` stream,
  so the check never blocks a frame and the call to action stays hidden on iOS
  and macOS until availability resolves to true.
- On iOS and macOS, `launchMobileLink`, assistant document links and Mastermind
  catch-up links refuse first-party purchase URLs: any `tuturuuu.com` path with
  a `pricing`, `billing`, `upgrade`, `subscribe`, `subscription(s)` or `top-up`
  segment, every `pay.tuturuuu.com` page and Polar hosts. Other links,
  including storefront and inventory checkouts that sell a workspace's own
  goods, are unaffected.
- On iOS and macOS, a locked feature says it is not available on the current
  plan without naming a plan to buy. Mira Live uses
  `assistantLiveTierUnavailable` for this.

## Regression coverage

- `apps/mobile/test/core/platform/purchase_channel_test.dart`: channel per
  platform and store availability, and the unavailable defaults.
- `apps/mobile/test/core/platform/purchase_entry_point_test.dart`: the call to
  action hidden until availability resolves, the native launcher, and the
  external channel elsewhere.
- `apps/mobile/test/core/platform/purchase_links_test.dart`: purchase URL
  matching and link refusal on iOS and macOS.
- `apps/mobile/test/features/assistant/widgets/assistant_live_purchase_gating_test.dart`:
  the Live tier message on iOS and macOS versus Android and Windows.
