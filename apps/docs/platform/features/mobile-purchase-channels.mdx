The Flutter app decides how a plan, credit or subscription purchase may start
through one seam, `PurchaseChannel` in
`apps/mobile/lib/core/platform/purchase_channel.dart`.

| Platform | Channel | Behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Android, Windows, Linux, web | `external` | Purchase links open the Tuturuuu web billing pages. |
| iOS, macOS (App Store builds) | `unavailable` | No external purchase link opens; upgrade entry points are hidden. |

App Store Guidelines 3.1.1 and 3.1.3 forbid the Apple builds from linking to a
purchase made outside StoreKit. When StoreKit purchases are wired,
`appleStorePurchaseChannel` becomes `native` and upgrade entry points call
`startNativePurchase` instead of a web page.

## Rules

- Paid entitlements a user already holds stay usable on every channel. Tier
  badges, credit balances and the current plan name are displays, not offers.
- Off the `external` channel, `launchMobileLink`, assistant document links and
  Mastermind catch-up links refuse first-party purchase URLs: any
  `tuturuuu.com` path with a `pricing`, `billing`, `upgrade`, `subscribe`,
  `subscription(s)` or `top-up` segment, every `pay.tuturuuu.com` page and
  Polar hosts. Other links, including storefront and inventory checkouts that
  sell a workspace's own goods, are unaffected.
- On the `unavailable` channel, a locked feature says it is not available on
  the current plan without naming a plan to buy. Mira Live uses
  `assistantLiveTierUnavailable` for this.
- Show an upgrade, plan or credits call to action only when
  `showsPurchaseEntryPoints(resolvePurchaseChannel())` is true.

## Regression coverage

- `apps/mobile/test/core/platform/purchase_channel_test.dart`: channel per
  platform, the native switch, purchase URL matching and link refusal on iOS
  and macOS.
- `apps/mobile/test/features/assistant/widgets/assistant_live_purchase_gating_test.dart`:
  the Live tier message on iOS and macOS versus Android and Windows.
