## What a Project holds

A Project belongs to one workspace. Every product labels it **Projects**. It
groups the work for one outcome:

- **Tasks**: from any board in the workspace. Tasks keep their ticket numbers
  and project history.
- **Meetings**: a Meet meeting shows its Projects under the meeting title, and an
  ended meeting shows them with its record. Tasks has no Project page yet, so the
  chips do not link.
- **Calendar events**: the link model already accepts events. The Calendar
  surface ships separately.

A meeting or event can belong to several Projects. An item and its Project are
always in the same workspace, and links never cross workspaces.

## Who can link

Two rights are needed to link or unlink an item:

1. **Manage projects** in the workspace.
2. Edit rights on the item in its own product:
   - **Meeting**: the meeting host, or a workspace admin.
   - **Calendar event**: manage calendar.
   - **Task**: the existing task rules.

The default is that only these people can link. Members with only one of the two
rights see the linked Projects, but no linking controls.

## Who can see links

Full workspace members see a meeting's Projects. Guests do not, even when they can
join the call. Removing a meeting or event removes its links, and deleting a
Project removes every link to it.

Links are stored privately and are read and written only through product servers.
The server applies these rules before any read or write, and the database keeps
every link inside one workspace.

## Reserved kinds

Drive files, Docs, Upskii classes and mail threads are reserved for later. Each
needs its own edit-right rule before it is accepted. No surface links them yet.
