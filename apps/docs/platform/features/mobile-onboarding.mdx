title: Mobile onboarding
description: When the Flutter first-run onboarding appears, what its three steps change, and the rules behind it.
audience: developers
---

The mobile onboarding (`apps/mobile/lib/features/onboarding`) replaces the
earlier five-slide carousel. It uses the Sorbet design: core/ui components,
`SorbetTokens` colours and the onboarding illustration scenes in
`lib/core/ui/kit/illustration/illustration_scenes_onboarding.dart`. It opens
above the app shell, never inside it.

The flow has three steps and a closing screen:

1. **Make it yours**: photo (the existing avatar picker and upload), display
   name (the profile API), language (EN or VI), appearance (Light, Dark or
   Auto) and Product news by email.
2. **Set up your space**: an open workspace invite with Join, then Just me
   (keep the personal workspace) or With my team (create a workspace by name
   through the same create action the workspace picker uses).
3. **What brings you here?**: Calendar, Tasks, Chat, Education, Finance and
   Mira tiles, multi-select.
4. **You're all set**: Add your first task, Connect your calendar, Turn on
   notifications, and Open Tuturuuu.

## Decisions

| Rule | Default | Surfaces | Exceptions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The flow runs once per account per device, after that account's first successful sign-in on the device. | Outcomes (`completed`, `skipped`) are stored per user id, so a second account added later gets its own run. | Mobile app router, after the auth, MFA and workspace redirects. | Accounts already on the device the first time the gate runs (the signed-in account and saved accounts) are recorded as `existing` and never see it, so an app update does not interrupt current users. Settings, Replay Tuturuuu guide, replays it for anyone and records nothing. |
| It never runs before sign-in, during MFA, in add-account mode or while switching accounts, and waits for a selected workspace. | The redirect requires an authenticated account with a workspace. | Router redirect. | Settings, the profile editor, the workspace picker and the telemetry prompt are never redirected. |
| A pending deep link or a tapped notification wins. | The link opens where it points; onboarding is deferred for the rest of the app session and shows on the next launch. | Deep links, push notification taps. | If the flow was already open when the link arrived, it closes without recording an outcome. |
| Every step and the whole flow are skippable, and nothing blocks the app. | Skip records `skipped` and continues to where the account was headed. | All steps. | A failed name save shows once; the next Continue moves on. |
| Product news by email is a separate opt-in, off by default. | Off. | Step 1. | No user-level product email preference exists in the backend yet, so the switch is shown disabled with a "Coming soon" note and nothing is stored. |
| Teach or learn sets the account's Education mode. | Finishing step 3 with Teach or learn sets the account mode to Education through `AudienceModeCubit.setAccountMode`; any other selection sets General. | Step 3. | Skipping step 3 leaves the mode unchanged. Workspace mode choices still take precedence. |
| Interest choices are mirrored to the web onboarding progress. | Saved locally under `connected_onboarding_interests:<userId>` and `connected_onboarding_goals:<userId>`, and sent as `goals` to `PATCH /api/v1/user/onboarding-progress` using the shared goal vocabulary (`focus`, `collaborate`, `learn`, `operate`). | Step 3. | The sync is best effort; a failure never blocks the flow. |
| The workspace limit is shown inline. | When the limits say no more workspaces can be created, With my team shows the limit under the name field instead of calling create. | Step 2. | API errors show their message in the same place. |
| The open invite card uses the notification inbox's invite. | The newest unanswered `workspace_invite` notification; Join accepts it the same way the inbox does and selects the workspace. | Step 2. | No card when there is no open invite notification. |

## Regression coverage

- `test/features/onboarding/onboarding_gate_test.dart`: once per account,
  existing accounts on first run, per-user outcomes, deep-link deferral.
- `test/core/router/app_router_onboarding_test.dart`: signed out, MFA,
  add-account mode, account switching, no workspace, exempt routes, pending
  deep link, already completed or skipped, a second account, safe return
  targets.
- `test/features/onboarding/onboarding_page_test.dart`: each step, skip at
  every step, replay records nothing, product news off and disabled, name
  save and failure, workspace create and limit, invite join, Education and
  General modes, goal sync, next-step links.
- `test/features/onboarding/onboarding_design_render_test.dart`: design
  renders of the four screens at phone and tablet, light and dark, plus
  Vietnamese step 1.
