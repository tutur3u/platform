## Product scope

Bill splits are private workspace records in standalone Finance, Finance in Web,
and the Flutter Finance app. Finance editors create a draft, choose workspace
contacts or members, enter expenses and payers, and preview each person's share
before finalizing. A contact without a linked workspace account can be included
in the calculation. A separately authenticated, authorized actor records any
payment on that contact's behalf. Membership changes take effect on the next
access check.

A split has one settlement currency. Each expense records its original currency,
payers, attendees, lines, adjustments, and, when needed, an explicit exchange
rate. Each finalized revision preserves those inputs and the normalized ledger.
Equal, percentage, and exact allocation apply to the whole expense or to each
item. An expense attendee can be omitted from individual items. Tax, tip, and
discount are allocated by each attendee's share of the expense subtotal unless
the adjustment specifies its own allocation. A full sponsorship covers the
beneficiary's remaining eligible share. A partial sponsorship moves a fixed
amount of that share to the sponsor. Sponsorship is a reassignment of cost. Any
reimbursement due to a sponsor is represented in the ledger and can carry a
direct payment requirement.

## Amounts and settlement

Compute and store integer minor units. Convert an expense with a pinned rational
rate in major units, accounting for both currencies' scales, and round its total
half up. Allocate rounding differences with largest remainders and stable
participant IDs for ties. Reconcile payer credits and share debits independently
to the same converted total. A draft cannot finalize when payers do not reconcile,
an exact split does not sum to its input, a sponsorship exceeds the remaining
share, or a discount leaves a negative share. Foreign amounts remain visible
alongside the normalized settlement amounts.

A confirmed transfer reduces the sender's debt and the recipient's receivable by
the confirmed amount. A report awaiting confirmation has no effect on balances.
Confirmed partial payments reduce any matching direct obligation before routing
the rest. Excess across a required pair remains a confirmed credit in the net
ledger. The plan reserves the unpaid portion of each mandatory direct transfer
and solves for the remaining net debtors and creditors. It counts distinct
directed pairs, coalescing an additional transfer on a reserved pair into the
same payment. The plan routes the residual directly from debtors to creditors
without asking a participant to relay payments. Finalized revisions and
confirmed payments are immutable. Corrections use a new revision or a recorded
reversal with its own audit event.

The solver explores residual balance states. For each possible debtor and
creditor it considers a transfer of the smaller remaining amount, then continues
with at least one endpoint cleared. An optimal residual flow can be made
acyclic by canceling flow around cycles without adding edges. Every acyclic
flow has a leaf whose transfer clears an endpoint, so searching these states
covers an optimal solution. Memoization reuses the optimal continuation. A
direct pair reserved earlier adds zero edges when reused. The endpoint and
search-work bounds keep the calculation predictable. When a bound prevents
proving optimality, label the feasible plan as a suggestion and retain the
direct obligations. Verified plans show the minimum within this routing policy.

## Data and permissions

Store split headers and revisions, identities, expense allocations, directed
obligations, payment events, comments, audit events, reminder jobs and link
metadata in the private schema with RLS and explicit role grants. A composite
workspace/split reference scopes each child; contacts and selected workspace
members are validated against the same workspace. Use transactional revision
checks and idempotency keys for mutations. Signed-in workspace members with
Finance access read splits they manage or have joined. Editors manage their own
splits. A linked debtor can report a payment and attach proof. Only the receiving
party confirms it. Private proof downloads authorize against the payment and the
current actor on every request. Comments and activity are visible only to split
members. Realtime signals carry a revision or event cursor; each client refetches
through its authorized API. Reminders run on Cloudflare with bounded retries,
recipient preferences, deduplication, and a durable cancellation fence when a
payment is confirmed, access is withdrawn, or reminders are disabled.

Contact bank details live in a separate private relation with explicit consent and
revocation history, independent of split display labels. Authorized members see
masked details. A reveal or QR render requires reassessing workspace access and
consent and records who accessed the account and why. Store bank identifiers and
account details privately, and keep audit rows free of raw bank values. Consent
withdrawal removes the usable details and invalidates QR access. Generate VietQR
locally from validated bank details, without sending the account number to a
third-party image generator.

## Links and entitlement

Canonical universal links open
`/{locale}/{workspaceId}/finance/bill-splits/{splitId}` on Tuturuuu, with optional
`/participants/{participantId}` and `/settlement` destinations. Standalone Finance
uses the equivalent workspace route under its Finance mount. The app verifies
the workspace, split, and target together before rendering. A signed-in link is
navigation and does not confer membership. It prompts for login on both free and
paid workspaces.

A paid workspace can opt into a separate read-only settlement status link.
Issue and redeem it only while the workspace passes the existing subscription
tier check and the public feature flag is enabled. Missing billing or guard
state fails closed. Use a random 256-bit token with a stored digest, expiry,
revocation, and participant or settlement scope. The public entry page accepts
the capability in the fragment and exchanges it in a same-origin request body
to keep it out of request URLs and referrers. Responses contain settlement
amounts and status plus explicitly chosen display names or nicknames. They omit
contacts, workspace identifiers, item descriptions, comments, proof and account
numbers. The link owner may explicitly include their own receiving QR. Show that
a payment app can decode the QR into account details before enabling it. Downgrade,
revocation, consent withdrawal and flag changes apply on the next request.
Keep redemption and its projection private with `Cache-Control: no-store`.

## Shared public abuse protection

Screen public requests before looking up split data. Use the existing
`tuturuuu-abuse-guard` service with trusted ingress identity, durable command
budgets, per-IP admission before lookup, and a second quota keyed by a verified
link digest. Require the existing Turnstile verifier on suspicious traffic,
binding successful challenges to the server-owned action and trusted origin.
Never allocate a per-link limiter to arbitrary supplied tokens. Denied,
missing, expired and revoked links share the same resource-not-found response.
Limit body size and reject malformed encodings before storage access. Exclude
raw tokens, proof references and sensitive fields from logs and telemetry.

Other public routes can reuse the admission adapter by supplying a fixed
surface ID, trusted source identity, a verified subject when available, and
surface-specific challenge policy. Stop admission durably before scheduling
further work when the budget or activation fence closes. Keep bank, anonymous
sharing and new public abuse call sites disabled by server-side flags until
independent security review and staging verification are complete.

## Verification

Allocation tests cover integer conservation, excluded attendees, percentage and
exact validation, sponsorships, multiple payers, adjustments and foreign rates.
Settlement properties compare the plan's edge count with an independent
brute-force oracle for small ledgers, and check conservation after direct and
partial payments. Database and API coverage must establish cross-workspace
denials, confirmation and retry behavior, bank and proof access, and anonymous
projection and entitlement boundaries. Native acceptance covers universal links,
authentication handoff and the Finance dock.
