description: 'Who can see a notification, when email-addressed invitations are claimed, and what happens on account deletion.'

Notifications are server-owned. Web, satellite and mobile clients read and update
them only through the notification API routes, which use the service role after
resolving the signed-in user. The `anon` and `authenticated` API roles hold no
privileges on `public.notifications`. The row-level policies are kept as defence
in depth.

## Who sees a notification

- A notification with a recipient account (`user_id`) is visible only to that
  account. Email is never matched on such rows, so later address changes or
  reuse of the address by another account do not expose it. New and updated
  rows do not keep a copy of the recipient's address; copies stored before this
  rule are inert and left for a separate batched cleanup.
- A row is addressed by email only while it is an **unclaimed workspace
  invitation** (`type = 'workspace_invite'` with no `user_id`). It is shown to
  the account whose **confirmed** address matches. Unconfirmed addresses never
  match.
- Addresses are stored trimmed and lowercased, and compared the same way.

## Claiming invitations

An unclaimed invitation is assigned to an account when:

- the account is created with an already-confirmed address (for example OAuth),
- the account confirms its address, or
- a confirmed account changes to a new confirmed address.

Only unclaimed invitation rows are claimed; rows that belong to another account
are never reassigned. `sync_my_notifications()` applies the same rule on demand
for the caller's confirmed address.

## Account deletion

Deleting a `public.users` row deletes that account's notifications instead of
leaving them without a recipient. The rollout migration removed rows left behind
by deleted accounts: recipient-less rows that were not invitations, and
recipient-less invitations with no pending email invite for that workspace and
address.

## Scope and exceptions

- Applies to every app that shows the notification inbox: web, satellites through
  the shared notification popover, and mobile through the API.
- The account email helper used by these checks, `public.get_current_user_email()`,
  resolves only the caller's own confirmed address.
- Delivery (email and push batches) is unchanged; batch rows keep the delivery
  address they need.
- Notifications are no longer published to Supabase Realtime; clients refresh
  through the API.
