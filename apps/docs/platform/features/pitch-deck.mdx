The localized `/pitch` page is a 29-slide presentation about Tuturuuu's ecosystem,
workforce decision support, and long-term intelligent-partner vision. It is excluded
from search indexing. Existing product foundations, proposed pilot work, and
long-term research are explicitly distinguished; product availability varies.

## Present

Use arrows or previous/next controls to navigate. Home and End jump to the first
and final slides. Space toggles twelve-second autoplay. Playback pauses when the
browser tab is hidden, notes open, or the presenter navigates manually. The overview
lists every slide. Fullscreen shows a translated error when unavailable. Motion
and optional closing-slide confetti respect reduced-motion preferences.

Stable hashes such as `/pitch#workforce`, `/pitch#simulation`, and
`/vi/pitch#pricing` support sharing. Existing slide hashes remain valid after
reordering. Print renders every slide with landscape page breaks. Presenter notes
are included in the public payload: they are not a private channel. Never include
confidential opportunities, private partner briefs, or unpublished commitments.

The capacity sandbox is a deterministic, synthetic illustration. It multiplies
team size by available hours and compares that result with estimated weekly work.
It does not run an AI model, persist inputs, collect telemetry, or recommend staff
allocation. Its limitations and baseline are visible. Interaction tracking in the
pilot narrative describes proposed development, not tracking performed by this page.

## Evolve

Edit the `pitch` namespace in `apps/web/messages/en.json` and `vi.json`, then run
`bun i18n:sort` and format the owned locale files. Keep locale shapes aligned with
`PitchCopy`. Each slide has a chapter, headline, narrative, public presenter note,
maturity label, and visual panels. Stable identifiers live in `pitch-model.ts`.
The presentation uses official branding tokens through `pitch-brand.ts`; specialized
art and the synthetic simulation live in the adjacent components and CSS modules.

The pricing slide and subscription calculator use the same public catalog API as
the pricing page. They refresh every five minutes and show an unavailable state
instead of a fabricated fallback. Distinguish current prices from proposed
allowances, annual charges from monthly equivalents, and subscriptions from custom
prototype scope. The engagement slide presents a budget structure, not a quote.

Do not republish legacy traction, fundraising amounts, partner endorsements,
certifications, savings, or delivery dates without current supporting evidence.
Pilot timelines are indicative, require scoping, and are not delivery commitments.
Workforce decision support must retain transparent assumptions and human review;
it is not a promise of autonomous employment decisions.

## Verify

Run focused pitch tests, `bun check`, and the real web build through
`ttr resources run`. Inspect both locales at desktop and mobile sizes, keyboard
navigation, deep links, overview, notes, simulation, reduced motion, and print.
Keep `/pitch` public in `apps/web/src/constants/public_paths.ts`. Route ownership
changes also require updating the TanStack migration manifest.
