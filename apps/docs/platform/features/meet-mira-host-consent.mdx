## Product decision

Decision (24 November 2026). Status: implemented.

Scope: Meet and Parley meetings with room Mira (room chat **@mira**), live
transcription and AI notes. Private Mira keeps its existing eligibility rules
(see [Meet Live assistants](/platform/features/meet-live-assistants)); only its
notice changes, as described under Intentional exceptions.

Default: off. A meeting uses Mira only after an eligible acceptor turns it on
and accepts the terms for that meeting.

| Rule | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Who accepts | Only the fixed session host or a co-host assigned in the room, while admitted and present. A workspace admin must join the meeting to accept. Guests never accept. |
| What the acceptor accepts | The Terms of Service and AI use for this meeting, the enabled capabilities, and confirmation that the meeting audience is 18 or older. The acceptor is responsible for that AI use in the meeting. |
| Acceptance evidence | Each acceptance is an append-only, revisioned record: acceptor account, meeting, server time, notice version, capabilities and revision. Turning Mira off, a new notice version, or any capability or visibility expansion (for example sharing notes to the workspace) ends the acceptance and starts a new revision. |
| 18+ | The meeting workspace needs its stored 18+ audience declaration; meetings in workspaces without it cannot turn Mira on. The acceptor's per-meeting confirmation adds to that declaration. There is no exception for minors. Education workspaces with learners under 18 do not make the declaration. |
| Notice | Every participant, guests included, sees **Mira is on, turned on by {acceptor}** (the acceptor's server-side profile name) as a persistent in-call indicator that cannot be hidden. Someone still in the lobby or the waiting room sees **Mira is on in this meeting** without a name: they are not a participant yet, so the name appears once they are admitted. Turning Mira on posts a system line in room chat. |
| Joining | The pre-join screen shows a tick box: **Mira, our AI assistant, may be turned on in this meeting to provide a helpful experience (notes, captions, answers).** Beside it, an info button explains what Mira does, who can read transcripts and notes (the host and workspace admins), retention, and how to turn Mira off at any time, with links to the Privacy Policy, the Terms of Service and the Mira AI page. The box is ticked by default; a workspace setting can make it unticked by default. Joining with the box ticked turns Mira on for you, including notes, captions and the live transcript: after admission it records your transcript Allow. Unticking it means Mira is off for you and records a transcript Decline, which pauses capture for everyone. Joining with the box unticked, whether unticked by the participant or left at an unticked workspace default, is recorded as an explicit off right after admission even when the host has not turned Mira on yet; a tick records nothing until a host acceptance exists, and then you confirm. When your own last choice in this meeting was off, the box starts unticked on a rejoin, on any device. The notice also says: *Mira is for people 18 or older. Under 18? Untick the box.* Mira off never limits anything else in the meeting. At join, the participant's final box state, the notice version, a hash of the shown text and the locale are recorded as that participant's own choice. |
| Mid-call changes | When Mira is turned on during a call, or the acceptance changes, everyone already present sees the same tick box and notice, preset to the workspace default, and confirms it. Until they confirm, they count as off. |
| Default setting | Workspace settings → Mira: **Mira consent box ticked by default** (on unless the workspace changes it). The setting needs `manage_workspace_settings`, records who changed it and when, and applies to joins after the change. |
| Evidence before effect | A participant's Mira on takes effect only once the server has stored that choice's evidence. The room holds the on as pending, with no effect on anything consent-gated (the person keeps their previous state and the call shows Mira as turning on), and the server confirms it after the write. A pending on that is not confirmed within 20 seconds is dropped, and so is one left when the room restarts or is restored; the person keeps their previous state and is asked again. Turning Mira off applies at once and cancels a pending on. |
| Per-person opt-out | At any time, a participant can turn Mira off for themselves. While it is off, their speech is not transcribed or sent to a model and Mira does not read their messages. **Mute Mira for me** (stop hearing Mira's voice replies) and **Leave** stay available. Only the participant can turn it back on. |
| Effect of one opt-out | Room Mira excludes only that participant's content and keeps running for everyone else. Live transcription pauses for everyone while anyone is off, because host capture cannot separate one voice from the room audio; nothing the participant says is captured or uploaded. |
| Guests | Guest choices are keyed to the server-issued guest participant id, never to a display name or label. A new guest join always starts without a choice. |
| Reporting a minor | Any participant can report that someone under 18 is present. The report ends the acceptance; turning Mira on again needs a new acceptance and new choices. |

### Required notice content

The notice (EN and VI) names the controller (the meeting workspace), the
processor (Tuturuuu), the model sub-processor (Google Gemini) and the
cross-border transfer; the purpose; the data types (speech, transcripts, chat
messages); retention (transcripts as configured, audio not kept); who can read
transcripts and notes (the host and workspace admins); the participant's rights and how to turn Mira off; and a reminder not to
share sensitive data. It also states two limits: other participants' speech or
messages that mention you are processed, and your voice can be picked up by
another participant's microphone.

The notice wording and the consent form are subject to legal review against
Vietnam's personal data protection rules before production use. Advisory: a
pre-ticked box can be weaker evidence of express consent under Decree
13/2023 than an unticked box that the participant ticks. The workspace default
setting and the recorded final state at join exist so a workspace can choose
the stricter form; counsel review covers which default production uses.
The host also says aloud, when the meeting starts, that Mira is on and how to
untick the box.

### Enforcement model

Consent is the participant's own choice. A host acceptance alone never makes
anyone's content available to Mira.

- **Room chat.** Admission and dispatch leases stay in the meeting room Durable
  Object. Each choice stores `{acceptanceRevision, noticeVersion}` and counts
  only while both equal the current values. Every choice and every acceptance is
  also written durably: host acceptances in a new
  `private.meet_mira_host_acceptances` table, and choices with an
  `acceptance_revision`. Both follow the `private.meet_ai_consents` rules:
  append-only, RESTRICT on meeting or workspace deletion, and account deletion
  anonymizes the account.
- **No choice means excluded.** A participant who joined before the acceptance,
  or has not chosen, is off.
- **Ending an acceptance** bumps the revision. Admitted dispatch leases are
  refused at their next step, and their answers are discarded and never
  published. Turning Mira on again does not revive earlier choices; everyone
  chooses again.
- **Opting out** applies the deferred-Pause behavior per person. New requests
  carrying that participant's content are blocked at once. In-flight answers
  that carry it are discarded, and their usage still settles. Requests that
  carry only other people's content continue.
- **Rejoining** keeps a choice only within the same acceptance revision and
  notice version.
- **Mixed sources.** A request whose sources include anyone no longer current is
  stale as a whole.
- **Context.** Context includes only content authored by participants whose
  current choice is on: messages, quoted and reply-to bodies, attachments, poll
  answers and reactions. Mira's own earlier replies stay omitted.
- **Transcription and notes** use host capture with per-speaker attribution.
  The host's device uploads short clips, and every clip names the account that
  spoke. Transcription runs only while every admitted participant has chosen
  Allow in the transcript consent notice. A decline, or anyone who has not chosen
  yet, pauses it for everyone, and audio heard while anyone was undecided is
  discarded on the device. The server refuses a clip without a speaker account, a
  clip whose speaker has no current Allow, and a clip from a session that is not
  the room's active recording. Turning Mira off for yourself also withdraws your
  transcript consent. Notes and summaries use only transcribed lines and never
  list opted-out participants as speakers. Erasing anyone's content redacts the
  whole mixed notes. Per-account capture (`self_v1`), where each account
  uploads only its own microphone, is a later hardening of this path.

Room Live voice (mixed room audio) remains denied on the server; this decision
does not enable it.

### Deleting a meeting or workspace

Scope: every Meet and Parley meeting whose Mira acceptances, choices or
per-speaker (`self_v1`) consent decisions exist when the meeting, its
workspace or the account's workspaces are deleted.

Default: the originals are removed and a minimal **proof of consent** is kept
for three years, then purged. The proof exists to demonstrate that consent was
given; it holds no content.

`RESTRICT` stays on the evidence tables, so a bare delete of a meeting or
workspace that holds evidence is refused: a delete path that does not erase
first fails closed instead of destroying evidence. The product's delete flows
call `private.erase_meet_mira_evidence(meeting, workspace, reason)` (exactly one
scope, service role only) in the same transaction as the delete. It copies each
host acceptance, each participant choice and each `private.meet_ai_consents`
decision of that meeting or workspace into `private.meet_consent_proofs`,
deletes the originals (choices, then acceptances, then per-speaker decisions)
and writes one receipt in `private.meet_mira_evidence_erasures` (scope, ids and
counts). Only the delete wrappers and deletion functions call it, inside the
transaction of their delete. A delete that fails afterwards rolls all of it
back, and a concurrent write fails the transaction instead of losing evidence:
each table's copies must match its deletions. Before copying, it key-share
locks the accounts the evidence names, in id order, so an account deletion
running at the same time either finishes first (the copies then see its
anonymized rows) or waits and then clears the new proof rows. A per-speaker
session whose capture is active or draining refuses its own delete, so the
meeting cannot be deleted until capture ends: the meeting API answers 409
`MEETING_DELETE_BLOCKED` (also for any other row that still references the
meeting), and Meet tells the person the meeting can't be deleted yet and to
end the transcript capture first, in English and Vietnamese.

A proof row holds only who consented (the account id, or the guest id), when,
the notice version, the consent text hash, the revision and the choice
(`accept` or `end` for a host acceptance, with what it covered; `on` or `off`
for a participant; `allow`, `deny` or `withdraw` for a per-speaker decision),
the per-speaker `room_seq` where present, the meeting and workspace ids as
plain values with no foreign key, the erase reason and time, and `purge_after`.
It holds no transcript, audio, name, email or guest label. The table is private,
service role read-only, append-only, and rows cannot be deleted before
`purge_after`. Deleting an account clears the account id and the per-meeting
pseudonym that could link its rows back to it from its proof rows immediately,
in the same trigger that anonymizes the other evidence, so an erased account
leaves no identifiable "who". A guest id is not an account and stays.

Retention: `purge_after` is the erase time plus three years, a column default
that a later migration can change. Consent proof is the one exception to the
30-day workspace-deletion purge in the
[data protection standard](/platform/data-protection-standard#intentional-exceptions),
on the legal basis of demonstrating consent. Exceptions: none for contents, which
are always removed. The purge of rows past `purge_after` is a bounded Cloudflare
job with a stop fence, delivered separately; until it ships, proof is kept past
its date.

The erase runs inside the owner workspace deletion
(`public.delete_owned_workspace`), the attendance-aware meeting and workspace
deletes, and two wrappers (`private.delete_meeting_erasing_mira`,
`private.delete_workspace_erasing_mira`) used by the flows that carry no
attendance intent: the meeting route while attendance is off, account
deletion's workspace cleanup, onboarding rollback, orphaned-workspace cleanup,
QA cleanup (which never deletes a Personal workspace) and the Cron Control QA
expiry cleanup. Account deletion alone only anonymizes the account in the
evidence and proof.

The Web and Cron Control callers can deploy before the migration that adds the
functions. When the function does not exist (PostgREST `PGRST202`, or 404 for
Cron Control), they fall back to the bare delete, which RESTRICT still guards,
so a deploy that runs ahead of the migration only loses the erase, never
evidence. The QA expiry cleanup (a Cron Control Worker) deletes through the same
function and fails closed the same way.

The room's own copy goes with the room: ending a room drops its Mira state (the
acceptor and every account's choice), since an ended room cannot run Mira.

Regression coverage: `apps/database/supabase/tests/erase-meet-mira-evidence.sql`
(a bare delete is refused; each delete flow keeps exactly the minimal proof with
no content columns and removes the originals; the receipt; anonymization of proof
rows on account deletion; proof immutable and kept until its purge date; a
failing delete keeps the evidence; the Personal guard; settings and grants kept;
client roles denied), `packages/realtime/src/meet/room-mira-end.test.ts` (an
ended room keeps no Mira state), and the web, Cron Control and API tests for the
wrappers, the meeting route and the QA cleanup.

### Affected surfaces

Meet and Parley: meeting settings (acceptance), the pre-join screen, the in-call
Mira indicator and chip, room chat, live transcription and notes. Web workspace
settings keep the workspace 18+ declaration.

### Intentional exceptions

- Private Mira is started by the participant and charges their own payer. In a
  meeting with a current acceptance, it shows the same notice, including its 18+
  wording, and needs no separate Continue. The pre-join **Join** never starts
  Private Mira by itself. Outside such meetings, its existing notice applies.

### Review and regression evidence

This decision changes the consent model. The implementation ships only after an
independent security and privacy review of the exact code. Required regression
coverage:

1. An acceptance with no participant choices allows no capture, dispatch, reservation or notes.
2. A participant who joined before a mid-call enable stays excluded until they choose.
3. Ending an acceptance during an admitted lease discards the answer without publishing it, and re-enabling needs new choices.
4. A notice version bump makes every choice stale.
5. An opt-out during a lease blocks new requests and discards in-flight answers carrying that participant's content, while others keep Mira.
6. Context excludes quoted and reply-to bodies, attachments, poll answers and reactions from participants who are off, and excludes Mira's own replies.
7. Two guests with the same label get separate choices, and a new guest starts without one.
8. A rejoin keeps a choice only within the same revision and notice version.
9. The server refuses a clip without a speaker account, a clip whose speaker has no current Allow, and a clip outside the active recording; audio heard while anyone was off or undecided never leaves the host device.
10. Turning Mira off for yourself withdraws your transcript consent and pauses transcription, notes never list opted-out participants as speakers, and an erase redacts the mixed notes.
11. A workspace without the 18+ declaration cannot accept, and neither can a guest or an absent admin.
12. A capability or visibility expansion requires a new acceptance.
13. Acceptance and choice evidence survives a room restore, is anonymized on account deletion, and blocks deletion via RESTRICT.
14. The tick box follows the workspace default (ticked unless changed), unticking records off, the final state, notice version, text hash and locale are recorded at join, and the EN and VI notice and info panel include every required item and link.
15. A participant who joins with the box unticked before any acceptance has an explicit off (revision 0, source join) recorded with the notice hash and locale, which declines the transcript; a ticked box records nothing without an acceptance.
16. Someone in the lobby or waiting room sees that Mira is on without the acceptor's name, and a read before admission returns only the open revision, the workspace default and the caller's own last choice.
17. A rejoin in a new acceptance starts the box unticked when the caller's own durable last choice in the meeting was off, and an unknown lookup starts it unticked.
18. A Mira on has no effect until the server confirms it after storing its evidence; a confirm for another room, account or notice, or one that expired or was superseded, changes nothing; an off cancels a pending on; and a pending on is dropped when it expires, when the room restarts and when it is restored (`apps/meet-realtime/src/room-do-mira-pending.test.ts`).

## Rollout fence for the two-phase Mira on

The Meet Worker accepts a room that still answers a Mira on immediately
(no `pendingId`) so the Worker and the realtime room can deploy separately.
For such an old room the Worker keeps the compensating off (`system_fallback`
choice row and an alert) when the evidence cannot be stored.

- Deploy the Meet Worker before or together with the realtime room, never
  after: a new room with an old Worker never receives a confirm, so a Mira on
  stays unconfirmed, expires after 20 seconds and the person stays off.
- Remove the fence when the realtime room with two-phase on runs everywhere:
  delete `room-mira-legacy-on.ts`, the compensating off and its alert, and
  stop writing `system_fallback`. The value stays valid for historical rows.
- Owner: the Meet per-speaker lane. A transient room failure on a re-ask can
  still trigger the compensating off for an old room, which is another reason
  not to keep the fence longer than the rollout.

## Production E2E

The `meet-flow` suite of the
[Upskii production E2E](/build/devops/upskii-production-e2e) workflow checks
