The Meet share dialog (`/meet/{wsId}/meetings/{meetingId}`, **Share meeting**)
shows one link, a copy button and **Show QR**. Eligible meetings get a short
`ttr.gg` link. Every other meeting shows the canonical
`https://tuturuuu.com/meet/{wsId}/meetings/{meetingId}` link.

## Product decision

| Rule | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Rollout | Off unless the Web server flag `MEET_SHORT_LINKS_ENABLED` is exactly `true`. Set it only once `ttr.gg` serves `/meet/{code}` from the native Shortener Worker. While it is off, everyone gets the canonical link. |
| Eligibility | The meeting host (`workspace_meetings.creator_id`) has a paid personal plan (Plus, Pro or Enterprise, from `getHostMeetingTier`), **or** the requester is verified Tuturuuu staff (`isVerifiedTuturuuuStaffSession` on the subject-bound Auth record). Ineligible users keep the canonical link. |
| URL scheme | `https://ttr.gg/meet/{code}`, where the code is 7 random base62 characters (about 3.5 × 10¹² values) drawn with uniform rejection sampling. Codes never contain meeting or workspace IDs. |
| Prefix | Only verified staff may drop the `/meet/` prefix (`ttr.gg/{code}`) or set vanity slugs. Paid users who are not staff always get `/meet/`. |
| Vanity slugs | Staff only. `[a-z0-9-]`, 3–40 characters, starting and ending with a letter or digit, no `--`, and no reserved word (`api`, `assets`, `meet`, `admin`, …, see `MEET_RESERVED_SLUGS`). Each meeting has at most 5. A vanity slug is unique across all of `ttr.gg`, either at the root or under `meet/`. Staff can rename or delete only their own vanity slugs. |
| Destination | Exactly the canonical join URL. A short link only redirects, and Meet's join authentication and access rules still apply. |
| Lifetime | Generated links expire **30 days** after the later of creation and the scheduled meeting time, and are renewed (at most daily) whenever an eligible member opens the share dialog. Vanity links expire **365 days** after they are set or renamed. Deleting the meeting deletes all of its links. Expired links resolve as not found and are purged the next time the meeting's links load. |
| Abuse limits | 20 generated-link changes and 10 vanity changes per actor per hour, counted from the audit log. These limits are best-effort under parallel requests. The database enforces the cap of 5 vanity slugs per meeting under a per-meeting lock. |
| Passwords | Meeting links never take a password. The join page owns access. |
| QR | Rendered client-side (`qrcode.react`) from the displayed link. It is dark on light in both themes and has a full-screen **Present** mode for scanning across a room. |
| Analytics | Click counts reuse the shortener's `link_analytics` rows. |

## Enforcement

- `apps/web/src/app/api/v1/workspaces/[wsId]/meetings/[meetingId]/short-links`
  serves `GET`/`POST` and `…/[linkId]` serves `PATCH`/`DELETE`. They require
  workspace membership and write with the service role.
- The database migration `20261121160000_meet_short_links.sql` adds
  `expires_at`, `meet_meeting_id` (cascading from the meeting) and
  `meet_link_kind` to `shortened_links`. Restrictive RLS stops clients from
  writing meeting rows. The `guard_meet_short_link` trigger allows root and
  vanity slugs only for verified staff creators, reserves `meet/` for meeting
  links, pins the destination to the canonical join URL, and caps vanity
  slugs per meeting. A check constraint keeps passwords off meeting rows, and
  the link-shortener password route ignores them.
- `private.meet_short_link_audit` records every create, rename and delete.
  Only the service role reads or writes it.

## Redirect runtime

`ttr.gg/meet/{code}` and expiry resolve only on the native Shortener Worker (see
[Shortener](/platform/applications/shortener)). The legacy runtime answers
`404` for two-segment paths and ignores `expires_at`. Until the migration
is applied, the native store retries a lookup without `expires_at` when
PostgREST reports the column missing (42703), so existing links keep
resolving. `/api/verify` also treats expired links as missing.
