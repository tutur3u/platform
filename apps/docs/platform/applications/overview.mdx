Use the [Apps gateway](https://apps.tuturuuu.com) to find routable products.
Workspace apps keep their own permissions and session requirements when you
follow a link. The directory below groups products by purpose; it does not imply
that every module is enabled in every workspace.

## Discoverability and privacy

Search indexes explicit public product and learning pages. Workspace content,
authentication, invitations, private shared resources, and buyer transactions are
excluded by the [indexing policy](/platform/features/search-indexing).
Public form indexing uses the author's existing form controls. Publishing a
world or storefront is distinct from making a workspace publicly accessible.
