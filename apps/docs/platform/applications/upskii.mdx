Upskii is a separate product at [upskii.com](https://upskii.com), built for
teachers and learners, with a Vietnamese-first experience. This page orients you;
accounts, availability and pricing are published on the Upskii site, and Upskii is
not a Tuturuuu workspace app.

## What it offers

| Audience | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| Teachers | Create a class (community or private), draft lesson plans, lessons, quizzes and tests with AI, review and edit every draft before use, and track student progress. |
| Learners | Join a class by link or class code, turn course materials into summaries, flashcards, quizzes and voice lessons, and ask the class AI teaching assistant questions. |
| Schools | A school-facing experience for institutions that adopt the platform. |

## How AI is used

- AI produces **drafts**; the teacher reviews and approves them before students see them.
- Each class can have its own AI teaching assistant that answers from that
  class's materials, and the teacher controls the source content.
- AI assists teachers and learners; it does not replace the teacher.

## Getting started

1. Visit [upskii.com](https://upskii.com) and choose the teacher or learner entry.
2. Teachers create a class and invite learners by link or class code.
3. Add materials, generate drafts, review them, and share with the class.

## Relation to Tuturuuu

Tuturuuu's own education tools are [Learn](/platform/applications/learn),
[Teach](/platform/applications/teach) and [Tutoring](/platform/applications/tutoring).
Those run inside Tuturuuu workspaces; Upskii is a separate product with its own
accounts and sign-in.
