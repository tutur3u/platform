The [personal data protection standard](/platform/data-protection-standard) records requirements. The [inventory](/platform/data-protection/inventory) describes source coverage. Documentation does not implement retention or certify legal compliance.

## Current limits

Complete discovery, export and erasure across all products, files, derived information, processors and backups have not been established by the source review. Individual deletion and export features cover subsets. Delayed synchronization, retries and restored copies must also respect withdrawal and deletion; a single successful product action does not prove that coverage.

## What completion requires

Rights requests must be fulfilled without undue delay and within the shorter applicable deadline described in the standard. Report missing outcomes, retained lawful subsets and expected expiry honestly. A request remains incomplete when required provider responses or deletion outcomes are unknown.

Implementation work requires separate review and authorization. Publishing this status does not activate proposed controls or authorize changes to live data. Engineering plans and qualification procedures are maintained privately.
