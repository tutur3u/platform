Uploaded files, images, attachments, recordings and associated ownership information support content delivery and collaboration. Upload progress information supports reliable transfers. Derived search information supports finding stored content.

See the [personal data inventory](/platform/data-protection/inventory) and [data protection standard](/platform/data-protection-standard) for scope, rights and retention limits.
