## Billing cleanup before deletion

Web account deletion and manual workspace deletion complete billing cleanup before deleting workspace or account records. A missing billing customer, a revoked or missing seat, and a missing or already canceled subscription count as completed cleanup.

Other provider failures, including network errors, timeouts and service failures, stop database deletion and return a temporary failure. Multiple active subscriptions block manual workspace deletion until the conflict is resolved. Existing permission, personal workspace and paid sole-member restrictions still apply.

## Privacy requests and completion

Billing blockers do not remove the right to submit a privacy request. Account removal and subscription cancellation do not prove complete erasure across files, derived content, processors or backups. The [personal data protection standard](/platform/data-protection-standard) defines applicable deadlines, scoped lawful exceptions and truthful reporting of incomplete work. See the [inventory](/platform/data-protection/inventory) for current coverage limits.
