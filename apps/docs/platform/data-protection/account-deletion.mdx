## Public deletion page

`/account/delete` on `apps/web` is the account-deletion URL in every store listing (`urls.accountDeletion` in `apps/mobile/store/metadata/*.json`), so it must work without signing in.

- **Signed out (default):** a static page names Tuturuuu and Tuturuuu JSC, lists the deletion steps, what is deleted and what is kept, and links to `/login?nextUrl=/account/delete`. It links `privacy@tuturuuu.com` for people who cannot sign in. The render makes no account, user or workspace lookups and reflects nothing from the request URL.
- **Signed in:** the page renders the existing deletion flow unchanged. The `/api/v1/users/me/delete` pre-check and deletion stay behind session auth, email confirmation, the paid-subscription block and the rate limit.
- **Retention copy:** the "what is kept" list restates the Privacy Policy retention terms (`packages/legal/src/documents.ts`, served at `/privacy`) and the current database behavior: deleting the auth user marks `user_private_details` as deleted and removes workspace memberships, while the `public.users` profile row remains. Update the copy in `account-deletion-public` (en and vi) whenever either changes.

The route stays on the existing `APP_PUBLIC_PATHS` entry; no proxy allowlist changes. Regression coverage: `apps/web/src/app/[locale]/(marketing)/account/delete/page.test.tsx`.

## Billing cleanup before deletion

Web account deletion and manual workspace deletion complete billing cleanup before deleting workspace or account records. A missing billing customer, a revoked or missing seat, and a missing or already canceled subscription count as completed cleanup.

Other provider failures, including network errors, timeouts and service failures, stop database deletion and return a temporary failure. Multiple active subscriptions block manual workspace deletion until the conflict is resolved. Existing permission, personal workspace and paid sole-member restrictions still apply.

## Privacy requests and completion

Billing blockers do not remove the right to submit a privacy request. Account removal and subscription cancellation do not prove complete erasure across files, derived content, processors or backups. The [personal data protection standard](/platform/data-protection-standard) defines applicable deadlines, scoped lawful exceptions and truthful reporting of incomplete work. See the [inventory](/platform/data-protection/inventory) for current coverage limits.
