Account and profile information supports sign-in and membership. Contacts, personnel, health and journal information supports the corresponding workspace features. Messages, documents, learning records and AI interactions support communication, collaboration and personalization. Billing and transaction records support subscriptions and accounting.

See the [personal data inventory](/platform/data-protection/inventory) and [data protection standard](/platform/data-protection-standard) for scope, rights and retention limits.
