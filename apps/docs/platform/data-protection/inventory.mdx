This inventory describes categories found in repository source. It does not establish that every feature is deployed, populated or configured identically. Retention and rights requirements are set out in the [personal data protection standard](/platform/data-protection-standard); this inventory does not certify complete enforcement.

## What data we hold

| Category | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Identity, profiles, memberships and preferences | Sign-in, account administration and workspace access |
| Contacts, personnel, contracts, compensation and attendance | Relationship and workforce management |
| Health information and personal journals | Health and private reflection features |
| Tasks, calendar events, time records and collaboration history | Planning, scheduling and work coordination |
| Chat, mail, notifications and connected-provider content | Communication and delivery |
| AI prompts, outputs, memories and usage | Inference, personalization and billing attribution |
| Meetings, recordings and transcripts | Calls, scheduling and transcription |
| Learner, parent, assessment and submission information | Teaching and learning |
| Forms and responses | Surveys and submissions |
| Financial, billing, order and inventory information | Accounting, transactions and subscriptions |
| Documents, files, media and publication history | Authoring, storage, sharing and publishing |
| Security signals, diagnostic logs and usage events | Abuse prevention, operational diagnosis and product metrics |
| Legal acceptances and consent evidence | Record the particular publication or processing purpose accepted |
| Development and operational records | Maintain and verify the service |

Free text, attachments and derived information may also contain personal data about people other than the account holder. Public sharing does not remove those people's rights.

## Retention and deletion limits

Product deletion and individual exports cover subsets of data. Removing an account, workspace or visible item does not by itself prove erasure of files, derived content, provider copies or backups. Some processing has expiry or cleanup behavior; this source review did not establish uniform expiry or complete export and erasure across every store.

Shared content, required financial records and other lawful exceptions need scoped decisions. Keeping a subscription record is not a general exemption for unrelated content. Requests and outstanding provider outcomes must remain visible until resolved. See [account and workspace deletion](/platform/data-protection/account-deletion) and [data protection delivery status](/platform/data-protection/delivery-plan).

## Connected services and local copies

Authentication, billing, messaging, AI, storage, telemetry and customer-connected services may process data for their respective features. Feature-specific consent and permissions apply. Browser and device caches, queued work, backups and external-provider copies have separate lifecycles; a product action alone does not prove all copies are gone.

## Effective legal publications

Live legal pages serve the revision actually in force. Proposed changes remain proposals until separately approved and effective; they do not rewrite acceptance history. Consult the [current privacy notice](https://tuturuuu.com/privacy) for published terms and the [data protection standard](/platform/data-protection-standard) for documented requirements and unresolved enforcement limits.
